Possibilistic Variational Inference

LPSM, France & NTU, Singapore · 04/2026 - 08/2026

Research internship supervised by Badr-Eddine Chérief-Abdellatif (CNRS, LPSM) and Jérémie Houssineau (NTU). Wrote reference notes on uncertainty modeling, variational inference, information theory, and possibility theory. Probabilistic Modeling

Variational Approximations of Gibbs Posteriors

Probabilistic Modeling · MVA

Reproduction and extension of Alquier, Ridgway & Chopin's PAC-Bayesian analysis of variational approximations to the Gibbs posterior, comparing two variational families against a reference SMC sampler on real classification datasets (breast cancer, Pima diabetes). The variational approximations match SMC test accuracy while running about twice as fast at high temperature.

Reported endpoints: SMC 0.75s to 11.5s; F2 (mean-field) ~4s and F3 (low-rank) ~5s, both flat

Adaptive MALA with Truncated Drift

Probabilistic Modeling · MVA

Reproduction of Atchadé's adaptive truncated-drift MALA algorithm, compared against standard MALA on three sampling targets, including a light-tailed distribution and an anisotropic Gaussian. On the light-tailed target, standard MALA stalls (0.000 acceptance rate) while the truncated adaptive version keeps sampling at 0.685.

Reproduced finding: adaptive T-MALA's sample covariance matches the true anisotropic target; standard MALA stays isotropic and misses the elongation
